# Hooks: the first 1–3 seconds

Part of the brain (start at `trends/BRAIN.md`). One lesson per line: the lesson, *why*, the tool to use, proof
`[views: source id]` (`./vtk kb show <id>`). Max 80 lines: merge or retire to `trends/archive.md` before adding.

- **Proof on frame 1, key message inside 3 s, outcome first**: "Your flight's gate in 2 taps". *Outcome/product-first
  hooks got ~2x the views of the weakest type; keep the key element centred and still for 1 s or more.* Tool: `kinetic`
  text, full size on frame 0. [[OpusClip, 34,635 TikToks, Apr 2026](https://www.opus.pro/blog/tiktok-hooks-that-go-viral-2026);
  [JAMS eye-tracking, Mar 2026](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11747-025-01137-x); dd3bqb]
- **Script the hook as a curiosity loop in 3 beats**: **context lean** (say the topic plainly so the right viewers
  self-select; pull them in with a benefit, pain, metaphor or surprising fact) → **scroll-stop interjection** (one short
  line with a contrast word: "But…", "However…") → **contrarian snapback** (same topic, the opposite direction). The bigger
  the snap, the stronger the hook, but the payoff must be on screen or viewers churn (your brand's versions: its MARKETING.md). [828K: lmxpbp]
- **Check every hook for the four killers**. A hook's one job is to make the right viewer opt in: topic clarity +
  on-target curiosity. **Delay:** the topic lands in the first 1–2 s (cut fluff; "you won't believe this" says nothing).
  **Confusion:** fewer, simpler words (5th grade or under: say what "profit" means instead of the word; MrBeast's Shorts
  read at ~1st grade; test: would a 10-year-old who barely speaks English get it?), active voice; if it reads two ways,
  rewrite. **Irrelevance:**
  you/your (not I/me/we) and a need-to-have pain, not a nice-to-know topic. **Disinterest:** contrast A (what they believe
  or do now) vs B (the better way); stated names both, implied names only B. Hooks are 2–3 lines: clarity, then contrast.
  [989K: 2bypp; 4.9M: as7abw]
- **Upgrade a topic hook into a belief hook** (good = attention, great = an unanswered question): name the hidden cause
  or flip the frame. ❌ "How to get more views" → ✅ "More content won't get you more views. Here's what will." ❌ "How to
  be consistent" → ✅ "You don't have a consistency problem. You have a system problem." ❌ "Storytelling matters" → ✅
  "Your story isn't boring. You're telling it in the wrong order." Or a near-miss gap: "I almost deleted the product
  that made me ₹1 lakh." Stack context (what is it about) + curiosity (why care) + an open loop (what do I need to know).
  Before posting: does it stop the scroll, is the topic clear, is there an unanswered question, a promise worth staying
  for? *Not louder, not more shocking: the viewer must want the next second.* [700 likes: dela58]
- **Visual hook first**: 3–5 bold words that set the topic on frame 1, over a visual with *just enough* motion (the 3D
  phone settle, a tap). Text + motion + voice together beat voice alone. Design the first frame before the words, like a
  long-form thumbnail: it should work as a title + thumbnail and make sense muted. [828K: lmxpbp; 4.9M: as7abw]
- **Series framing**: one fixed first-frame layout per series: the place or thing people already know (the restaurant,
  not her face) + the item + "$1 burrito" dead centre, the same every episode, so viewers recognise it and binge the
  playlist. [4.9M: as7abw]
- **Write 10 hooks, test the best**: "how would [big creator] say this?" for ~10 big creators, pick favourites, twist
  them into your own, test 2–3 on non-followers (Instagram Trial Reels) and keep the best viewed-vs-swiped.
  [4.9M: as7abw; [Meta, Dec 2024](https://about.fb.com/news/2024/12/trial-reels-try-content-non-followers-first-see-what-perfoms-best/)]
- **Lead with the benefit or pain, then the thing** ("If you want better sleep, you need magnesium"). **Borrow
  familiarity** (airplane mode, Apple's privacy label, the Notes app, the junk drawer). **First value within ~4 s**;
  staccato hook sentences, longer ones later. [828K: lmxpbp]
- **Recognisable moment**: "When the group chat asks who still owes money". *Viewers see themselves in it.* Tool:
  `problem-payoff` / `pov` templates. [baseline]
- **Ask the question the last line answers, and make it the title**: "Can you cook faster than fast food?", "How many
  flavors can you get with $1?", "What's faster: X or Y?". Said from 0.0 s (43 of 61 narrated hits say the title in the
  first 3 s). Variants: "I'm doing X with $0", a label ("One dollar boba."), "My mom thinks X. But what she doesn't
  know…" (a secret the viewer shares). *The viewer knows the game in 1–2 s and must stay for the answer.* [top 80, up to 185M: jennyhoyos]
- Also works: a specific number, a direct command, a contrarian line (only if the video proves it), or a guess the
  viewer commits to ("How long does it take to find X?") broken by the real answer on screen. Hook text 6–10 words,
  inside the 3:4 crop, said by the VO too. **Avoid** "Hi everyone", "Okay so…", "Have you ever wondered…", opening on
  the app name or logo. [baseline; a number in 32% of top hooks: jennyhoyos; 311K: m2oygu]
- **Curiosity frames are prefixes, not hooks**: the viral "10 hooks that always work" list (Nobody mentions this · I wish
  I knew this earlier · Pause for a second · Ever noticed this pattern? · Here's the real truth · Let me save you hours ·
  This may surprise you · You need this now · You may not agree with this · I just figured this out). *Alone each is the
  delay killer; glued to the topic in the same breath it adds a promise:* "Nobody tells you this about your mom's insurance
  card." First 2–4 words only, the topic by second 1.5. [49K saves: screenrecording-10-04]
- **Spot-the-difference hook**: two near-identical things side by side with A / B tags, the outcome gap as a real number
  ("One of these got 7× more views than the other"), then "Can you see why?". Rule out the obvious next with ticks (same
  topic ✓ same words ✓ same layout ✓: "only one thing is different… and it's not what you think"). *The viewer starts
  playing in second 1, every tick narrows the search, and the answer is the last beat.* Tools: two `card` image overlays
  with A / B `mark_label` pills, `mark: check`, `counter`. [5.1K likes: dechlq]
- **The viewer's own artefact with one gap**: their plan / checklist on a phone with every item ticked except one
  highlighted "???" row, and the line "Here's what your plan is missing…". *It names who it's for and opens a loop the
  whole video fills.* Tool: an `iphone` clip of a real checklist (or `checklist-grid`) + a `highlight` mark on the blank
  row. [screenrecording-10-08]
- **Sell the emotion around the chore, never the chore**: in the family-paperwork space the tips videos run 10–30x
  smaller than the feelings around them: mental-load couple comedy ("My wife knows everything 😆" 8.7M, 100K+ shares,
  sent to the partner), "one home for all your important documents" show-and-tell (8.5M), parents getting older
  (2.1–2.4M, comedy works), "if something happened to you, would they know?" (1.1–2.7M). Lead with the feeling, let the
  product be the relief. [8.7M: family-app-niche-social-study; 1.8K likes, 8.3K comments: dbk2yf]
- **Relief-flex hook + proof pile-up**: "The reason I don't have to [remember everything]… I have a [system] for that."
  One calm line on frame 0, then on the song's drop (~2.2 s) the real system floods in: screenshots stacking every ~0.5 s,
  each one more proof that everything has a place; 8 s total, no voice. The caption carries the teaching (the reframe "the
  goal isn't remembering 700 things, it's not expecting your brain to hold them", the old way named: "dig through email,
  the camera roll") + a comment-keyword freebie. Short video = claim + proof; long caption = the argument. Tools: one
  `hook` text, `card-wall` / stacked image overlays on the drop. [1.8K likes, 8.3K comments: dbk2yf]
- **Aim one step away from the core pain**: money / time / health / access said straight trips the BS detector; name the
  tactical pain next to it and let the viewer make the leap ("make better videos" sells "make money"). Twist the knife
  (the frustration, bluntly), then show the gap between where they are and where they want to be. [257K: g-6tiu]
