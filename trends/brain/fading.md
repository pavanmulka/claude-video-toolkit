# Fading: what stopped working

Part of the brain (start at `trends/BRAIN.md`). One lesson per line: the lesson, *why*, the tool to use, proof
`[views: source id]` (`./vtk kb show <id>`). Max 80 lines: merge or retire to `trends/archive.md` before adding.

- The baseline list lives in CLAUDE.md ("Fading"): Hormozi neon captions, constant caps, glitch or flash on every cut,
  logo openers, over-polished ads, watermarks, near-identical templated uploads, stock AI voices. [baseline]
- Engagement-bait clips ("SUBSCRIBE! U MAY NOT SEE ME AGAIN", 53M) still pull creator views but read as spam for a brand. [jennyhoyos]
- **Polished creator-demo product videos** (a creator unboxes / tours the product with a white text box): worked for
  family hardware brands in 2025 (1.8–3.4M hits), median fell from 2–5K to ~500 views in 2026 while daily screen memes
  kept growing. [family-app-niche-social-study]
- **The "a friend says you need [app]" story ad**: a 63 s AI-illustrated story with AI faces and dialogue captions (the
  pain is real: "the second opinion needs the first", "my history is in three WhatsApp chats and a cupboard I'm scared
  to open"), the app named by a friend at 34 s, a logo end card: 16 likes. *The pain lands, then the ad trope and the
  length lose it.* Keep the pain line, drop the story: the product in the first third, 15 s, no AI people. [16 likes: dy6ywd]
