# Product reveal, proof, CTA & offers

Part of the brain (start at `trends/BRAIN.md`). One lesson per line: the lesson, *why*, the tool to use, proof
`[views: source id]` (`./vtk kb show <id>`). Max 80 lines: merge or retire to `trends/archive.md` before adding.

- The product appears after the problem lands. Show real UI (no mock-ups pretending to be the app). [baseline]
- **One CTA per post, matched to its goal** (pick the goal first): follows → a reason to follow · comments → an easy
  but meaningful question · saves → something worth revisiting · shares → name who should receive it · sales → a
  natural next step, never desperate. Two asks split the action. [74K comments: ddnlim]
- Soft CTA; a question to viewers drives comments. For ads, CTA end cards lifted recall. A comment-keyword freebie
  ("Comment 'Formats' and we'll DM it") drew one comment per four likes, a diagnosis carousel's "Comment VALUE for the
  free guide" two per three (1.4K on 2.2K), a "comment DROPS to join" carousel more comments than likes (7.4K on 7K),
  an 8 s reel's "Comment PHONE for my free guide" 4.6 comments per like (8.3K on 1.8K);
  it needs an auto-DM tool and a real freebie, best the next step of what the post just taught (the fix guide after a
  diagnosis). [74K comments on 37K likes ("comment MASTER"): ddnlim; 8.3K comments on 1.8K likes: dbk2yf; 7.4K
  comments: demb4r; +6]
- **Privacy apps: prove the architecture, use the label as backup.** ~42% of labelled apps claim "Data Not Collected" and
  Apple doesn't verify labels; privacy TikToks get fact-checked in public. Show no account, no company servers, works in
  airplane mode. [[arXiv 2025](https://arxiv.org/abs/2206.02658); [PoPETs 2024](https://petsymposium.org/popets/2024/popets-2024-0111.php);
  [TechCrunch on Stardust, 2022](https://techcrunch.com/2022/06/27/stardust-period-tracker-phone-number/)]
- **FOMO only with a real limit, after the why.** Fake scarcity is deceptive; "free" pulls far harder than cheap; buyers
  want the reason first. [[FTC, 2022](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2022/09/ftc-report-shows-rise-sophisticated-dark-patterns-designed-trick-trap-consumers);
  [Marketing Science, 2007](https://pubsonline.informs.org/doi/10.1287/mksc.1060.0254); [TikTok Next 2026](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/en/next);
  urgency you don't believe sounds fake, always-on offers stop working: 311K: m2oygu]
- **A specific outcome + a named method, never a vague promise.** "We help you get organized" gets compared with
  every other app (and on price); "X in 2 taps, organized by people" stands alone. Name *how* you do it so it can't be
  mistaken for the rest. [311K: m2oygu]
- **Answer obstacles, shrink time and effort.** List the audience's 5–6 obstacles; every proof shown answers one of
  them (one obstacle per video). Then say how soon it works and how little they do ("set up in 2 minutes, nothing to
  sign up for"): the shorter the time-to-result and the lower the effort, the cheaper the ask feels. [311K: m2oygu]
- **End on a picture metaphor, logo last**: the facts build to one visual reframe (a door: "The audience you're looking
  for… is in a different room", it swings open and the camera flies through), then a ~2 s logo build on the same stage.
  *The metaphor is the snapback in picture form; the logo arrives after the payoff, never before.* Tools: `door-reveal`
  (the doorway is transparent: the next scene shows through), `logo-reveal`. [screenrecording-10-08]
