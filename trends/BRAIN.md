# The brain: what makes short videos work (read this first)

Learned from every video, talk and article shared with Claude (list: `SOURCES.md`). Generic on purpose: a brand's own
plans live in its private workspace. Read this page before planning, grooming or scripting any video, then the pages
the job needs. Limits keep it sharp: this page 40 lines, each topic page 80 (`./vtk kb` shows the usage).

## Golden rules (the lessons proven most often)
1. **Frame 1 is the thumbnail**: 3–6 bold words that name the topic, readable with the sound off. [hooks]
2. **The hook is the question the last line answers**, said from 0.0 s; the pain or outcome first, never the logo. [hooks]
3. **Lean → "But…" → snapback**, and the payoff is proven on screen, or viewers leave. [hooks]
4. **Talk from frame 1 and never pause** (~3.6 words/s), in words a 10-year-old gets. [narration]
5. **Promise the ending in the first 3 s**, re-hook at ~70%, and end on the payoff words (no outro). [narration]
6. **A new picture every 1.5–3 s**, one focal point each; proof moments play at real speed. [structure]
7. **Show visible progress** toward the promised end: a timer, a tally, a checklist. [structure]
8. **Loop it**: the last frame leads straight back into the first. [structure]
9. **Let the sound make the promise and the picture break it.** [motion-sound]
10. **Real proof, never mock-ups**; the product shows up after the problem lands. [product-cta]
11. **Plan the week 3-2-1**: 3 topic groups × 2 videos + 1 test; drop a group that flops for 2 weeks. [strategy]
12. **Own a lane**: stack differences, borrow formats from far-away niches, never clone the leader. [strategy]
